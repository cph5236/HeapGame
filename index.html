Heap

A shared pile of trash.
Everybody climbs it.

How it works

Climb Scale a mountain of junk. How high you get is your score.
Leave something Where you fall, you drop an item. It stays in the world.
One heap Every player climbs the same pile. The ladder at 400m was put there by someone else.

The heap only grows. Nothing you add is ever removed.
