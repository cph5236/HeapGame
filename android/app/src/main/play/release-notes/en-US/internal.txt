Heap has a new app icon.

Speed items now boost you in the air, on wall slides and on ladders, not just on the ground.

Steering along a wall now builds up, so pushing toward an opening actually carries you into it.

Bonking your head on a slope now glances you off to the side instead of stopping you dead.

Tapping Play Again after an ad no longer starts your run behind the ad.

Players in Europe, the UK and Switzerland can manage ad data choices under Privacy options in Settings.
