# Privacy Policy

**App:** Heap
**Last updated:** August 20, 2026

---

## Overview

Heap is a mobile 2D platformer where players climb a community-built trash heap. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how it's handled. We keep it simple because we collect very little ourselves — but the Google Play version of the game shows ads, and advertising involves third parties. That is covered in [Advertising and Consent](#advertising-and-consent) below.

---

## What We Collect

### Data you provide
- **Display name** — a name or nickname you choose for the leaderboard. You can use any name; we do not require your real name.
- **Feedback and bug reports** — if you use the in-game feedback button, we receive the message you type, along with the diagnostic details listed below.

### Data collected automatically
- **Score and gameplay data** — your high score, height reached, and related in-game statistics, used to populate the leaderboard.
- **Device-generated identifier** — a random GUID generated on your device the first time you play. This is not tied to your identity and is used to associate your scores with your name across sessions.
- **Diagnostic data** — when the game hits an error, we record the error message and stack trace along with your random GUID, a per-session ID, the app version, the platform (Android or web), and your browser/device user agent string. This is used to find and fix crashes.

### Google Play Games Services (if signed in)
If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, we receive your Google Play Games player ID and display name from Google. This is subject to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Signing in is optional — the game is fully playable without it.

### Advertising (Google Play version only)
The version of Heap distributed on Google Play shows ads through Google AdMob. Google collects your device's **advertising ID** and ad interaction data to serve and measure those ads. See [Advertising and Consent](#advertising-and-consent).

The web versions of Heap (heapgame.com and itch.io) contain no ad code and collect none of this.

---

## What We Do NOT Collect

- Real name, email address, or contact information (unless you type one as your display name or in feedback)
- Location data — *we* never collect or receive it. Ad partners may use location data for advertising, depending on your consent choice; see below.
- Device hardware identifiers (IMEI, MAC address, etc.) — the advertising ID used for ads is resettable and controlled by you in your device settings
- Photos, microphone, or camera access
- Payment or financial information
- Browsing history or data from other apps

---

## How We Use Your Data

| Data | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Display name | Show on the in-game leaderboard |
| Score / gameplay stats | Populate the leaderboard; track your personal best |
| Random GUID | Link your scores to your display name across sessions |
| Google Play Games ID | Authenticate your session (if you sign in) |
| Diagnostic data | Diagnose crashes and errors |
| Feedback message | Read and act on your bug reports and suggestions |
| Advertising ID | Serve and measure ads (Google Play version only, handled by Google) |

We do not sell or rent your data. We do not share your leaderboard, gameplay, or feedback data with advertisers. Ad-related data is collected by Google AdMob and its ad partners directly, as described below.

## Advertising and Consent

The Google Play version of Heap shows two kinds of ads through **Google AdMob**: full-screen interstitial ads between runs, and optional rewarded video ads you choose to watch in exchange for in-game rewards. There are no ads in the web builds.

To serve these, Google and its ad partners may store and access information on your device — including your advertising ID — and use it to personalize and measure ads. This processing is carried out by Google and those partners under their own privacy policies, not ours. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [how Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

### If you are in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland

Before any personalized ads are shown, the game presents a consent dialog from Google's certified consent management platform. You can:

- **Consent** — ads may be personalized using your advertising ID.
- **Do not consent** — you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized.
- **Manage options** — choose which purposes and partners you allow individually.

The dialog lists the ad partners involved; the full list is available from the "List of partners" link inside it.

### Changing or withdrawing your consent

If you were shown the consent dialog, you can change your choice at any time, and as easily as you gave it:

> **Settings → Privacy options**

Selecting it reopens the consent dialog with your current choices, and any change takes effect immediately. The entry appears only where consent applies — if you were never asked for consent, there is nothing for it to change, and it is not shown. Withdrawing consent does not remove ads — it stops them being personalized.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID at any point in your device's Android settings, independently of this game.

## Data Storage

Your game data (name, scores, heap entries) is stored on our backend, which runs on Cloudflare's infrastructure. Cloudflare may process data in data centers globally. See [Cloudflare's Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) for details on their data handling.

Your local save data (placement history, settings) is stored on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Advertising data is not stored on our backend. It is held by Google and its ad partners.

---

## Data Retention

We retain your leaderboard entry and scores for as long as the game is operational. If you want your data removed, contact us at the email below and we will delete your records.

---

## Children's Privacy

Heap does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The game does not require any personal information to play. If you believe a child under 13 has submitted personal information (e.g., a real name as a display name), contact us and we will remove it promptly.

Heap is not directed to children, and is not marked as child-directed for advertising purposes.

---

## Your Rights

You may:
- **Access** your data — contact us to receive a copy of data associated with your GUID or display name.
- **Delete** your data — contact us and we will remove your leaderboard entries and save data from our servers.
- **Correct** your display name — you can update your display name in the game settings at any time.
- **Withdraw consent** for personalized advertising at any time via **Settings → Privacy options**, as described above.

---

## Security

We use HTTPS for all data in transit. Our backend is protected by rate limiting and secret-gated admin routes. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable precautions appropriate to the sensitivity of the data (which is low).
